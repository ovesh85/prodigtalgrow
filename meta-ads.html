Meta Ads FAQs

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We run campaigns for businesses in India, the USA, UK, Canada, Europe and the GCC.

Do you only manage Meta Ads?

Meta Ads is a core service, and we also handle landing pages, creatives, Google Ads, SEO and LinkedIn when they support your goals.

Do you produce the ad creatives?

Yes. We plan the creative strategy and can produce videos, UGC-style ads, carousels and statics through our product video team.

Can you take over existing campaigns?

Yes. We start with a free campaign assessment, then fix tracking, structure and creative before scaling.

Do you guarantee results?

No. Results depend on your offer, market and budget. We guarantee a clear strategy, disciplined testing and transparent reporting.

What budget do I need?

It depends on your goals and market. We'll recommend a realistic testing budget during the free consultation.
