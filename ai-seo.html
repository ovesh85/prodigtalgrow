How we get you found and cited by AI

AI SEO is an advanced extension of traditional SEO, not a replacement for it. It builds on solid SEO foundations and adds the layers AI search platforms rely on.

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

Optimize website content to improve visibility in AI-generated search results across platforms like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity.

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

Structure content to provide direct, relevant answers to user queries and improve visibility in AI-powered answer engines.

Structured Data & Schema Markup

Implement relevant schema markup to help search engines and AI systems understand your website content, services, products and business information.

Entity-Based SEO

Strengthen brand identity and authority through entity optimization, semantic relationships and consistent business information.

Semantic Content Optimization

Create context-rich content around search intent, related topics, entities and conversational queries.

AI Search Visibility

Optimize website content to increase the chances of being discovered, referenced and cited by AI-powered search platforms.

E-E-A-T Optimization

Technical SEO for AI Crawlers

Ensure proper crawlability, indexability, website structure, XML sitemaps, robots.txt configuration and accessibility for relevant AI search crawlers.

Conversational & Long-Tail Keywords

Target natural-language queries and question-based searches used in voice and AI-powered search.

AI SEO Performance Tracking

Monitor organic visibility, AI search mentions, brand citations, keyword rankings and website traffic.
