Product Video FAQs

Do I need to ship my product?

For studio shoots, yes. For AI product videos we can often start from your existing high-quality product photos.

How long does a project take?

Most projects take 1–3 weeks depending on scope. AI concepts can be ready within days.

Which formats will I receive?

A master film plus cutdowns in 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9, and ad variants for Reels, Stories and Feed.

Can you also run the ads?

Yes. Our Meta Ads team can launch, test and scale the creatives for you.
