LinkedIn Management FAQs

Do you manage both personal profiles and company pages?

Yes. You can choose personal profile management, company page management, or both together for the strongest results.

How many posts do you publish each month?

Our plans include 12–16 posts per month, planned in a monthly content calendar you approve.

Do you write the content?

Yes. We research, write and design the posts. You review and approve before anything goes live.

Will the content sound like me?

We start with a voice and positioning session and refine with your feedback, so posts sound like you, not an agency.

Do you handle engagement?

Yes. We engage with your network and target audience to grow visibility and start conversations.

Do you guarantee followers or leads?

No. We don't promise guaranteed followers, impressions, leads or revenue. We commit to consistency, quality and transparent reporting.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes, we work with founders and companies across India, the GCC, UK, USA and Europe.
